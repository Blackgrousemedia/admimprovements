About Us

Here at ADM Improvements we are experienced building and renovation specialists, improving homes across South East England for over 10 years.

We deliver high-quality workmanship on everything from full refurbishments and structural alterations to extensions, conservatories, windows and doors.

We have a trusted team of Kitchen and Bathroom fitters, along with electricians, plumbers and various other trades. A friendly, family run business, we strive for the best customer satisfaction and feedback.

We are available for both domestic and commercial work, our client base includes both local and national companies. We supply and install premium, A-rated products including uPVC windows, front and back doors, patio doors, bifolding and French doors, all designed to enhance security, energy efficiency and performance.

We can offer a huge range of composite doors, made to your specifications, as unique as your home.

Based in Maidstone, Kent, we’re a small, professional team known for our friendly approach, attention to detail and commitment to completing every project to the highest standard.Call or email us for a free, no obligation, quotation.
